3. Convert Raw Data into a Readable Format

llvm-profdata merge -output=app.profdata default.profraw

4. View the Expensive Functions
Now, use llvm-prof or llvm-cov to generate a clear, human-readable breakdown of function execution counts:

llvm-profdata show --topn=20 app.profdata

This prints the top 20 functions ranked by how frequently they were called. Alternatively, to see line-by-line execution counts directly inside your source code, run:

llvm-cov show .\my_program.exe -instr-profile=app.profdata
